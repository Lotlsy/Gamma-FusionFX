About Precious Metals

XAU — Gold Priced per troy ounce in USD. The ultimate safe-haven asset, used as a store of value and inflation hedge for millennia.
XAG — Silver More industrial than gold — roughly 50% of demand comes from manufacturing. Historically more volatile but higher upside.
XPT — Platinum Rarer than gold in the Earth's crust. Heavily used in auto catalysts; price often tracks manufacturing cycles.
XPD — Palladium Critical for gasoline car catalytic converters. Supply is concentrated in Russia and South Africa, making it geopolitically sensitive.
